University Health Centre / Clinic

The University Health Centre is a health facility serving the University of Kabianga community.

📍 Location

The Health Centre is located to the left of the Main Gate when entering the University Main Campus.

🧭 Finding the Health Centre

Enter the University through the Main Gate and look to the left side. The University Health Centre / Clinic is located in that direction.

🏥 Medical Emergency Safety Tips

  • Remain calm and assess the situation.
  • Contact the Health Centre or emergency services.
  • Provide your exact location.
  • Do not move an injured person unnecessarily.
  • Follow instructions from trained emergency personnel.

🔥 Fire Emergency Safety Tips

  • Raise the alarm immediately.
  • Leave the building using the nearest safe exit.
  • Do not use elevators during a fire emergency.
  • Move to the designated assembly point.
  • Do not return to the building until authorized.

🛡️ Security Emergency Safety Tips

  • Move away from danger to a safe location.
  • Contact campus security as soon as possible.
  • Avoid unnecessary confrontation.
  • Provide accurate information about the incident.
  • Follow instructions from security personnel.
